Safety and privacy

  • SuperSurf makes local, explainable observations. It does not bypass paywalls, CAPTCHAs, security warnings, consent choices or access controls.
  • There are no analytics, remote scripts, cookies or page uploads. Selected text opens an explicit Google search only after you choose the context-menu action.
  • The page health check reports browser-visible timing and resource signals; it is not a definitive diagnosis.

Remove SuperSurf

Chrome requires you to confirm removal in its extensions manager. SuperSurf cannot remove itself silently.
